AI moves fast, and keeping up by hand costs hours. So I built a bot that does the reading for me.

Every day it pulls from **Reddit, Hacker News, RSS, X and YouTube**, then uses an LLM to turn the day's signal into one briefing that lands in Telegram. It runs **unattended on a Linux VPS**, and I'm one of its daily users.
