I built the **content production system** for a brand: lessons, social posts and ads, all produced from the same shared visual and voice rules.

The key design decision was **structure over tooling**. The files are organised and versioned so they can be handed to different AI tools, whether Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, and still produce consistent, on-brand output. Two review stages keep drafts from ever mixing with approved work.
